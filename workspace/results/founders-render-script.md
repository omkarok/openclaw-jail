## SCENE 6: What Reactive AI Gets Wrong
**Visual:** Left side shows great answers trapped in chat history.
**Narration:** Reactive AI can produce brilliant outputs that die inside chat windows. No state machine. No retries. No ownership. No continuity. You get insight, but not guaranteed execution.

**Visual:** Right side shows lifecycle: pending -> in_progress -> done/failed + escalation cards.
